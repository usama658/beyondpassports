    Questions

    The ones you're already thinking.

    Still unsure? Message us. We reply within 30 minutes, no payment, no commitment.

    01 Can I apply from the UK on a Skilled Worker, Graduate, Student, Family or other UK visa? +

    Yes, with the right documents. Being on any of these visas does not stop you applying for a Schengen visa. You need a few extras most people miss: evidence your UK leave covers your full trip, and sometimes a letter confirming you will return to the UK.

    Message us your visa type →
    02 Do I have to pay just to ask? +

    No. Message us and ask. We reply within 30 minutes, no payment and no commitment. That is usually how it starts anyway: people check slots and stay because they realise how much the documents matter too.

    Check current slots →
    03 I travel in 3 weeks. Is there still time? +

    3 weeks is tight but workable for most destinations. The constraint is appointment availability. Italy is often booked weeks out; Spain and France usually have slots within 7 to 10 days. Message us first and we'll tell you honestly before you commit.

    Check my timeline →
    04 I was refused before. Can you still help? +

    Yes. Send your refusal letter and we identify exactly what the embassy flagged and what needs to change. In our experience most refusals are documentation errors, not eligibility problems, so they are usually fixable.

    Send my refusal letter →
    05 How do I know you're legitimate? +

    Fair question. Beyond Passports Ltd is registered at Companies House (17331903, verify at find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk) and ICO registered (ZC197159). Address: Unit 82a, Mildenhall, Suffolk IP28 7BG. Every testimonial has a reference number, email us to verify any.

    06 I'm visiting more than one Schengen country. Which embassy? +

    You apply through the embassy of the country where you'll spend the most days. If nights are equal, apply through your country of first entry. Applying to the wrong one is a common refusal trigger, so we confirm the correct embassy for your exact itinerary.
