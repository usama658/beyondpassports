  • ✕Accommodation booked before the visa arrives. Wrong sequence, instant red flag.

Yes, with the right documents. Being on a Skilled Worker, BRP, Graduate, Student or Family visa does not stop you applying for a Schengen visa. 06 I'm visiting more than one Schengen country. Which embassy do I apply to? +

You apply through the embassy of the country where you'll spend the most days. If nights are equal, you apply through your country of first entry. Applying to the wrong one is a common refusal trigger, so we confirm the correct embassy for your exact itinerary.
