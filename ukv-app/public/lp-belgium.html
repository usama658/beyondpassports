      Belgium visa FAQ

      Asked every week, answered straight.

      What we do, what it costs, and what we won't promise. If yours isn't here, just ask.

      01How do I get a Belgium Schengen visa appointment from the UK?+

      Belgium uses TLScontact centres in London, Manchester and Edinburgh. We complete your visa application, then watch the TLScontact calendar and take the earliest date that fits your travel.

      02How long does a Belgium visa take from the UK?+

      The consulate's published processing time is 10 to 15 working days after your biometric appointment, longer in peak season. Appointment lead time comes on top, so start 6 to 8 weeks before travel where you can. We cannot speed up the consulate's decision, only how fast your file is ready and submitted.

      03I'm on a UK residence permit. Can I apply for a Belgium visa from the UK?+

      Yes. If you legally reside in the UK you apply from the UK, whatever passport you hold. Your UK immigration status documents form part of the file, and we check them before anything is submitted.

      04What does the Belgium visa cost in total?+

      Three parts: the consulate fee of €96 for adults (paid to the consulate), the TLScontact service charge (paid to TLS, confirmed at booking), and our £158 fee, of which £109 is only due once your appointment is booked.

      05What travel insurance do I need for Belgium?+

      Schengen rules require medical cover of at least €30,000, valid across the whole Schengen Area for your full stay. A compliant certificate is part of the file pack we prepare.

      06I was refused a Schengen visa before. Does that affect a Belgium application?+

      A refusal sits on the Schengen system for five years and every consulate can see it. It does not block a new application, but the new file has to answer the refusal reason. We review the refusal letter first and tell you honestly whether and how to reapply.

      07I'm self-employed. What extra documents does Belgium ask for?+

      Typically your last tax return or SA302, business bank statements, and proof of your business (Companies House entry or invoices). We give you the exact list for your situation and check each document before submission.

      08When are Belgium appointments hardest to get?+

      Spring and summer, and the weeks before school holidays. The TLScontact calendar releases dates in batches, so empty today does not mean empty tomorrow. That is why we watch it rather than asking you to keep refreshing.

      09Is this actually a real, registered company?+

      Yes. Beyond Passports Ltd is registered at Companies House and ICO registered, both checkable yourself in seconds. Verify it, then message Sarah directly, she's online now and replies within 30 minutes, no payment, no commitment.

      11What's the one thing people usually get wrong without realising?+

      Small cross-document mismatches: a date that doesn't match between your passport and employer letter, the wrong residence-permit field, a passport number mistyped (an I read as a 1 is a real one we've caught). None of these feel serious until the consulate cross-checks and flags them.
