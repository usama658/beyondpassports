      Switzerland visa FAQ

      Switzerland questions, answered straight.

      What we do, what it costs, and what we will not promise. If yours is not here, just ask.

      01How do I book a Switzerland visa appointment in the UK?+

      Switzerland appointments run through VFS Global in the UK, including their London centre, not TLScontact or BLS. You need a completed application and reference before the calendar will show dates, and VFS releases those dates in batches. We get your file appointment-ready and point you to the earliest slot that fits your travel, so you are not refreshing the portal alone.

      02How long does a Switzerland visa take from the UK?+

      The consulate's standard decision time is around 15 calendar days from submission, and up to 45 in busier or more complex cases. Appointment waiting time sits on top of that. We can speed up how quickly your file is ready and submitted, never the consulate's decision itself.

      03I'm on a UK residence permit. Can I apply for a Switzerland visa from the UK?+

      Yes, provided your UK residence is valid, such as a Skilled Worker visa, student visa or settled status. A standard visitor visa does not count as residence. We confirm your status from your share code first, because getting this detail wrong is a common cause of refusal.

      04How much does a Switzerland visa cost in total?+

      Three separate parts: the consulate fee and the VFS service charge, both paid directly to them and confirmed at booking, plus our service, which starts from £49. If we review your case and cannot help, we refund you.

      05What travel insurance do I need for Switzerland?+

      Cover of at least €30,000 for medical costs, including emergency repatriation, valid across the whole Schengen Area for your full trip. A compliant certificate is part of the file pack we prepare.

      06I was refused a Schengen visa before. Does that affect a Switzerland application?+

      A prior refusal sits in the shared Schengen system for five years and must be disclosed; hiding it is worse than the refusal itself. Switzerland looks closely at your purpose of trip and your reasons to return, which is where most refusals come from. We build the new application to answer the exact reason you were refused, and we will tell you plainly if reapplying now is not the right move.

      07I'm self-employed. What extra documents does Switzerland ask for?+

      Usually proof the business exists, your recent tax records, and business bank statements covering the last few months. We give you the exact list for your situation and check each one before it goes in.

      08When are Switzerland appointments hardest to get?+

      Spring and summer, and the run-up to school holidays. VFS releases dates in batches, so empty today does not mean empty tomorrow, which is why we watch it rather than asking you to keep refreshing.
