E3. Calendar strip

Start-by dates are the event date minus 15 days processing minus the appointment lead a person saw on our board; where no lead was seen we say so and check for you.

How a trip idea works. You book hotels and ground transport directly with one licensed operator we introduce by name; we send that operator nothing about you, and we take no commission or referral fee on the trip. Beyond Passports sends you the visa file and that introduction, nothing else. We do not sell flights, hold money for hotels or transport, or combine services at one price. Appointment dates are set by the visa centre and visa decisions by the consulate. Travel insurance is required for every Schengen application.
