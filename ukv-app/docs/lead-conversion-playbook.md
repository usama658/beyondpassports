# Lead Conversion Playbook — inbound → paying client (Beyond Passports)

How to turn an inbound enquiry (WhatsApp / form / email) into a paying, well-served client
**without over-promising**. Pairs with `lead-refusal-recovery-playbook.md` (that one = how to
read + fix a refused case; this one = the sales/onboarding journey around it).

> **Golden rules (never break, at every stage):** We are an independent visa consultancy,
> **not the government**. We prepare + check the application and book the biometric appointment;
> **the consulate decides**. Never guarantee an outcome, an approval, or a date. Never promise to
> "speed up" a government decision. Never tell a client to hide a refusal or apply to the wrong
> country to get a faster slot. Honesty converts better than hype and protects the business.

---

## 0. The funnel at a glance

| Stage | Goal | Owner SLA |
|-------|------|-----------|
| **1. Capture** | Log the lead the moment it lands | immediate |
| **2. Acknowledge** | Human reply, set expectation | within 2 hours |
| **3. Qualify** | Get the 5 facts, judge feasibility | first reply / first call |
| **4. Consult (call)** | Diagnose, build trust, be honest on timeline | same day / next day |
| **5. Quote** | Scope + fee + what's included/excluded | on/after the call |
| **6. Close** | Agreement + deposit + intake email | while momentum is warm |
| **7. Onboard** | Docs to `documents@` → Drive folder → review | on payment |
| **8. Deliver + nurture** | Do the work, keep them updated, ask for review | through to outcome |

Conversion is won or lost on **speed of first reply** and **honesty on the call**. Slow = cold.
Over-promise = refund/complaint later.

---

## 1. Capture (immediate)

Every enquiry gets a row in the **Leads** tab (Google Sheet) the moment it arrives — even before
you reply. Columns: Date · Name · Phone · Nationality · UK status · Destination(main) ·
Prior refusal · Trip dates · Location · Deadline · Refusal reason · Status · Next action · Notes.

Set **Status = New** and **Next action = Acknowledge**.

---

## 2. Acknowledge (within 2 hours)

Calm, human, no promises yet. Purpose: stop them shopping elsewhere + open the qualify loop.

> "Thanks for reaching out. I can help look into this. So I point you the right way, could I ask
> a few quick questions? Then I'll be honest with you about what's realistic."

Then move straight into qualify. If they came in hot (refusal + near trip), offer a call:

> "This is easier on a quick call — when suits you in the next hour or two?"

---

## 3. Qualify (the 5 facts)

Collect before quoting anything (same list as the refusal-recovery playbook):

1. **Nationality**
2. **UK status** (student / PSW-Graduate / Skilled Worker / spouse / ILR …) + BRP/share code
3. **Passport expiry** (valid ≥3 months beyond return, issued within 10 years, 2 blank pages)
4. **Trip dates + the country of the MOST days** (decides where to apply)
5. **If refused before:** photo of the refusal letter + which country refused

**Feasibility gate — do this maths before you sell anything:**
- Schengen processing ~**15 working days** from biometrics, sometimes longer.
- Appointment must sit **~1–2 weeks before travel** for processing + passport return.
- Count working days from **today** to the trip. If it doesn't fit, **say so** and pivot to
  later dates — do **not** take money for a trip that can't realistically make it.

---

## 4. Consult — the call

This is where trust (and the sale) is built. Structure:

1. **Listen** — let them tell the story. Refused clients are anxious; acknowledge it.
2. **Diagnose out loud** — "One reason was ticked (box 10). Here's what it means, and it's
   recoverable." Naming the exact ground = instant credibility.
3. **Be honest on timeline** — if the near-term trip can't clear, say it now, then offer the
   realistic path (shift dates / next window). Honesty here *raises* close rate.
4. **Show the plan** — the concrete rebuild (full itinerary booked, matching cover letter,
   funds, return-intent pack). They're buying the plan, not a promise.
5. **Set scope + next step** — "I'll send you what's included and the fee. If you're happy,
   we start once you send your documents."

Do-say / don't-say (full list in the refusal-recovery playbook):
- **Do:** "the consulate decides"; "this ground is recoverable"; "I'll be honest if the timeline
  doesn't work"; "you must declare the previous refusal."
- **Don't:** "we'll get you the visa"; any guaranteed approval/date; "we can speed up the
  government"; anything implying we're official.

---

## 5. Quote

Send after the call, in writing (WhatsApp/email), plain and specific:

- **Tier**: refusal-recovery / tight timeline = **Advanced / bespoke** (manual review,
  refusal-ground analysis, cover-letter strategy, appointment booking).
- **What's included**: document review, cover-letter + itinerary strategy, form check,
  appointment booking, submission-ready pack.
- **What's separate / excluded**: the **consulate/VFS fee** (paid to them, not us), travel
  insurance, flights/hotels. State the service fee **does not guarantee approval**.
- **Timeline**: realistic window, tied to appointment availability.

One clear number. One clear next step. No fake urgency ("only today") — real deadlines only
(their trip date, appointment availability).

---

## 6. Close

- Confirm scope + fee agreed (in writing).
- Take deposit / payment via the proper channel (payment link / invoice — **never** ask for
  card numbers over chat).
- Send the **intake email ask** immediately (see stage 7) while they're warm.
- Update Leads: **Status = Won**, **Next action = Awaiting documents**.

**Objection handling:**
| Objection | Honest response |
|-----------|-----------------|
| "Can you guarantee it?" | "No one honestly can — the consulate decides. What I guarantee is a properly built, submission-ready file and honest advice." |
| "Too expensive." | Break down what's included vs doing it alone; the cost of a *second* refusal. |
| "Cheaper agent said yes-guaranteed." | Anyone guaranteeing approval is misleading you; that's a red flag, not a bargain. |
| "Can you make my trip next week?" | Run the maths openly; if it can't clear, offer the realistic date instead of taking money. |
| "I'll think about it." | Agree a specific follow-up time; send the plan summary so it sells itself. |

---

## 7. Onboard (on payment)

- Ask the client to email documents to **`documents@beyondpassports.co.uk`**
  (subject: their name + case). Verified working forwarder → `hello@` master inbox.
- Save every file into their folder under **`Beyond Passports - Leads/<Name> (<phone>) - <date>/`**
  in Drive, organised into subfolders (Application, Flights, Hotels, Financial, UK status, etc.).
- Review against the strong-file checklist (refusal-recovery playbook §6). Flag gaps early.

---

## 8. Deliver + nurture

- **Proactive updates** — short status message at each milestone (docs received / pack ready /
  appointment booked / submitted). Silence kills trust and invites chargebacks.
- **After outcome** — regardless of result, close the loop honestly. On approval, **ask for a
  review** (consent + anonymise per our testimonials policy).
- **Feed outcomes back** — log the refusal ground + what fixed it, so the playbook sharpens.

---

## 9. Follow-up cadence (don't let warm leads go cold)

For leads that don't close on the first touch:

| When | Touch |
|------|-------|
| +1 day | "Any questions on the plan I sent?" |
| +3 days | Add value — a relevant tip about their ground, not just "you free?" |
| +7 days | "Still keen? Slots for your window are moving — happy to hold your place." (only if true) |
| +14 days | Soft close — "I'll leave this here for now; message anytime." Mark **Status = Dormant**. |

Never pester. Value or a real reason each time.

---

## 10. Channel discipline (WhatsApp anti-restriction)

The lead number got **temporarily restricted** for looking like bulk/automated outbound. To
avoid it recurring:

- **Space out** new-contact first messages — don't fire many back-to-back.
- **Personalise** every first message (no identical copy-paste blasts).
- Save the contact **before** messaging where possible.
- If restricted: you can still **answer calls + reply in existing chats**; just **stop starting
  new chats** until it lifts. Don't tap around the timer.
- For volume outbound long-term, move to the **WhatsApp Business API** (compliant broadcast) —
  the personal app is not built for it.

---

## 11. Lead statuses (Leads tab)

`New → Acknowledged → Qualifying → Consult booked → Quoted → Won / Lost / Dormant → Delivering → Closed`

Update **Status** + **Next action** at every touch so any team member can pick the case up cold.

## Worked example — Bandita Deb (Greece refusal recovery)

- **Capture/Ack:** inbound WhatsApp, refusal + near trip → acknowledged, offered specialist call.
- **Qualify:** Indian, UK **PSW**, passport valid to 2031, trip **Greece only** (Corfu/Athens/
  Santorini) 5–15 Aug, refused **box 10** (purpose/conditions not reliable).
- **Consult (honest timeline):** today is late Jul, Greece needs ~15 working days from biometrics
  — **the 5 Aug trip cannot clear**. Pivot to a later window (mother in UK until 18 Sep).
- **Diagnose:** box 10 driven by an itinerary not fully booked (only Corfu confirmed at refusal) +
  an extended multiple-entry request that undercut a first-time PSW applicant.
- **Plan (what they buy):** book every night + all inter-island transport; single-entry,
  dates-matched; cover letter rewritten to match bookings 1:1; return-intent pack (employment
  letter + payslips + tenancy + PSW BRP); new dates that clear processing.
- **Onboard:** full document set received at `documents@`, filed to her Drive folder (7 subfolders).
- **Next:** verify funds (bank balance vs ~€50/day + costs), confirm final dates, issue quote.
