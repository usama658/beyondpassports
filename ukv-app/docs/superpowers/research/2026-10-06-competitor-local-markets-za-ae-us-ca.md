# Local competitor landscape: ZA, AE, US, CA (Schengen visa assistance), 2026-10-06

Agent: general-purpose. Method: ~35 WebSearch sweeps + ~55 WebFetch reads. WebSearch does not expose ad/organic labels. Anything not read directly on a page is marked unverified. Part of the 2026-10-06 competition-led research set. Hypothesis tested: real competitors in each new market differ from the UK auction set. RESULT: confirmed, ~80% of each market's set is not on the UK list.

## Cross-market synthesis
1. OVERLAP with UK locked set: only IAS (ZA + US subdomains, NY office) and Visard (US alerts/auto-book) appear abroad. Atlys present in AE/US as blog + appointment tool only; no resident-specific Schengen checkout confirmed in any of the four (unverified). iVisa ZA blog in USD. GoVisa, Tourloom, FlyFast, Breakout, Panelx, SVC: ABSENT in all four. ~80% of each market's set is off the UK list.
2. HIDDEN CROSS-MARKET PLAYER: WE ARE LONDONERS LTD (UK, OISC F202200007) runs 5+ lookalike domains selling US + CA Schengen appointments with dated promises ("7 Working Days"), WhatsApp/toll-free. A UK competitor using BP's intended playbook, already in two of four markets.
3. Strongest patterns to match: ZA = multi-city offices, Google review volume, in-person accompaniment. AE = published AED fee (450-899) with govt split, licence + IATA TIDS on page, guaranteed-appointment in tours (Musafir). US = pay-after-success booking (Visard), Telegram alerts, embassy-accreditation language (CIBT). CA = "Early appointment" named SKU at CAD 150-180, VFS-centre landing pages with fee tables (schengenvisapro).
4. GAPS NOBODY FILLS: (a) per-country VAC routing clarity in ZA (VFS/TLS/BLS/Capago/Visalink/GVCW, no agency names them); (b) ASATA/TICO/DET-type registration as selling point in ZA/CA; (c) transparent all-in pricing in ZA/US/CA (only AE does it); (d) refusal-aware consulting (only Avenue Professional/Noble Bridge in AE mention appeals); (e) HUMAN SERVICE THAT ALSO RUNS SLOT-MONITORING (bots have no humans, agencies no bots); (f) French-language Montreal/Quebec PR market: zero agencies; (g) honest anti-fraud positioning aligned with VFS "no fast-track" messaging; (h) Hellopeter-era VFS anger in ZA.
5. OPENNESS (most open first): CANADA (broken micro-agencies, no trust marks, consulates calling agents scams, strong PR-holder demand) > USA (fragmented, no national human brand for non-citizens; bots strong, agencies weak; H1B/green-card demand; TLS/BLS/VFS complexity) > SOUTH AFRICA (established offline agencies but zero pricing/VAC transparency, no ASATA play; Capago/TLS fragmentation = wedge) > UAE (most saturated: dozens of licensed agencies with AED prices, WhatsApp-first, tour bundling, guaranteed-appointment claims; price pressure to AED 450).
6. GAPS THE OWNER MISSED vs UK list:
   - WE ARE LONDONERS LTD multi-domain network (US/CA).
   - Slot-alert SaaS as a competitor class (Visard, Visa Catcher, Telegram/GitHub bots) in US/CA/AE.
   - Licensed Dubai travel agencies (Green Apple, Regal, AFC, GlobalVisaShop, Visa Guy, Musafir).
   - ZA offline agencies (One Visa World, Visa Logistics, Visa24HR, Visa Box, The Visa Agent).
   - Diaspora tour operators bundling visas (Akshar/Global Holidays NJ; Spring Travels/Journey Asia ON/BC).
   - Global corporates (CIBT, VisaHQ) ranking in US/CA.
   - DIFFERENT VAC OPERATOR PER MARKET (Capago/Visalink/GVCW in ZA; BLS=Germany in US; Cox & Kings Italy in UAE): UK slot board + TLS-centric ops knowledge do not transfer as-is.
   - Trust-mark expectations differ (DET/DTCM in AE, BBB in US, ASATA/TICO untapped): UK ICO/CH signals mean little locally.
   - Pain channels = Reddit/Blind/Hellopeter/Gulf News, not Trustpilot.
