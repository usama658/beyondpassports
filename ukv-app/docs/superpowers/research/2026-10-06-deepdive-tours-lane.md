## 3. How the visa is woven in
- Musafir (strongest): "we guarantee your Schengen visa appointment"; "no scrambling for slots". Yet tour page excludes visa; offer page "Schengen visa assistance AED 195", "visa application fee is non-refundable in all cases, including if rejected", "solely at the discretion of the Consulate", "complete visa formalities up to 30 days prior", "10-15 working days". Visa Protection Plan optional, "refunds on rejection subject to terms"; snippets "up to USD 100 per traveller, 36 destinations" [unverified]; India version ₹149 e-visa. Net: guarantee = appointment slot, not approval; VPP caps at a fraction of the Schengen fee.

## 4. Compliance signals (quotes)
- UK PTR 2018 reg 2 (legislation.gov.uk): travel services = carriage, accommodation "not intrinsically part of carriage", car rental, "any other tourist service". Accommodation + other tourist services NOT a package only if they "do not account for a significant proportion of the value" and are not "an essential feature", or are "selected and purchased after performance has started". A combination IS a package if "purchased from a single point of sale", "offered, sold or charged at an inclusive or total price", or "advertised or sold under the term 'package'". IMPLICATION: hotel + ground transport at one price under the word "package" = PTR package -> organiser liability + insolvency protection, even with no flights. ATOL only engaged by flights [general knowledge, verify].
- iVisa (EU 2015/2302 model): "This is a package within the meaning of Directive (EU) 2015/2302"; "iVisa is the organizer and is responsible for proper performance"; "provides insolvency protection ... Refund of payments ... Repatriation ... Continuation of travel".
- Travel Chasers: "ABTA (No. Y6593) and ABTOT (No. 5517)". Trafalgar/Costsaver: ABTA/ATOL/IATA logos, Guernsey address.
- Tourloom: company number, VAC non-affiliation, "We do not issue visas"; NO ATOL/ABTA/PTR language despite hotel packages.
- FlyFast: "private independent travel services company"; no company number/ATOL/ABTA. Breakout: "consultancy fees are separate"; no company number. EuroPath: flight-inclusive packages with no ATOL shown -> likely non-compliant.
- UAE: AFC "IATA Accredited Agent | DED & DTCM Licensed"; others not rendered. DET/DTCM licence for outbound tour operation [unverified].
- ZA: Flight Centre Reg 1994/000253/07 + ASATA/IATA; ASATA voluntary.
- US: oag.ca.gov "California requires all sellers of travel to register"; "display the registration number on all advertising"; Global Holidays shows no CST. CA reaches out-of-state sellers to CA residents [general knowledge, verify].
- Canada: Spring Travels "TICO Registration No: 50027501"; tico.ca: "Registered TICO agencies ... provide additional consumer protection that may not be available when booking with agencies based in other countries or provinces".

### 6c. Indicative price construction (honest "from")
1 cheapest twin on a NAMED 3★/4★ hotel for the shoulder week you will quote (bedbank or Booking.com public rate); log hotel, date, rate, screenshot date in sheet. 2 named ground transport at published public fare (Trenitalia/Renfe/SBB/DB/ferry), class named; private transfer at supplier quote. 3 breakfast only if included, else "room only". 4 hotel + transfers / 2; rail per person. 5 handling margin as separate INTERNAL line, never hidden in a "govt fee"; round; label "from ... per person, two sharing, {month} departures; peak dates higher". 6 show "Indicative, checked {date}" on page (none of 20 competitors do). 7 convert once from EUR at stated rate; no stale cross-market re-quotes. 8 flights, visa fee (€90), insurance, tips, lunches/dinners, city tax out, stated.

### 6h. Exposure flags
- PTR 2018 (UK): hotel + transport at one price = package -> organiser + insolvency protection (bond/trust/insurance) unless agent for a named organiser or genuinely separate purchases (then possibly an LTA, still needs protection). Single "from £X" total nudges toward package status. Mitigation: quote hotel and transport as separate supplier lines, paid separately; avoid the word "package" in UK copy ("trip plan" / "itinerary"); lawyer confirms.
- ATOL: not triggered while no flights sold or facilitated (no flight-search widget).
- UAE: outbound tour-operator activity likely needs DET licence; UK entity selling hotel packages to UAE residents = grey [counsel].
- US: CA/FL/WA/HI seller-of-travel reach out-of-state sellers; CA requires CST on all advertising [verify before US pages].
- CA/Ontario: TICO for sales to Ontario consumers; out-of-province online sellers grey [verify].
- ZA: ASATA voluntary; CPA pricing disclosures [verify].
- Visa claims: no "guaranteed appointment", no success percentages (Ads policy).
