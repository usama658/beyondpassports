## 6. G4 Tours: what to list and how
Card: {Country} · {N nights} · {cities} · hotel tier · NAMED ground transport · "Schengen visa preparation by Beyond Passports, fee quoted separately" · "From {local} per person, two sharing, {season}" · "Not included: flights, visa fee, insurance, lunches/dinners, city tax" · "Indicative, checked {date}".
