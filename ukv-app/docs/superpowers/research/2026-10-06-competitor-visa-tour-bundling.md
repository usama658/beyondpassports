Part of the 2026-10-06 competition-led research set (see also: intl-architecture, local-markets, seo-footprint, scaling-feasibility reports in this folder). ## 1. flyfastholiday.co.uk (FlyFast Holiday)
- Product mix: visa consultancy first, holiday packages + flights second. Nav: Home / About / Services / Holiday Packages / Flights / Blog / Contact. "Complete Schengen Support Package" bundles hotel reservation, itinerary, insurance, "flight assistance" into the visa fee. Flights page = ~13 routes, "BOOK NOW" goes to an enquiry form, not a booking engine.
- Packages: /holiday-packages/ 5-card grid: destination + days + "Starts with - £X", no inclusions, no cities; CTA goes to /request-visa-assistance/ (visa form). Examples: "Italy, 5 Days, Starts with - £150"; "Austria, 8 Days, Starts with - £222"; Slovenia 7d £195, Switzerland 6d £205, Germany 6d £215. These are visa-service prices dressed as holiday cards. Flights "from £547" UK-Nigeria.
- Visa hook: /uk-to-france-schengen-visa/ H1 "Prepare for Your France Schengen Visa With Clear Guidance". Tiers: "Appointment Support Package £100 -> £69 (31% OFF)"; "Complete Schengen Support Package £150 -> £119 (20% OFF)"; split "£60 upfront + £59 after appointment confirmation". No "guaranteed appointment" wording.
- Sales: two-stage form, WhatsApp, phone +44 749 035 1660, email. No checkout. T&Cs: "reservation is secured only after full or partial payment".
- Compliance: no ATOL, ABTA, PTR wording, no company number on site. Footer: "private independent travel services company ... not a government website". Refunds: "service fees and non-refundable deposits are not eligible for refunds." Two addresses (167-169 Great Portland St W1W 5PF; Unit 501 Leroy House N1 3QP). Companies House via search: FLYFAST HOLIDAYS LIMITED 16835827, inc. 6 Nov 2025, SIC 79110.
- Markets: UK residents only (form asks UK status BRP/BRC/PR/ILR/Citizen). No market pages.
- URLs: WordPress. /{country}/ (27), /uk-to-{country}-schengen-visa/ (9 money LPs), /holiday-packages/, /flights/, /services/. Template junk indexed (/slider-block/ etc.).
- Copy: £60/£59 split + "Complete" tier bundling hotel+itinerary+insurance into one visa price. Avoid: holiday cards priced at a visa fee with no inclusions; no company number.

## 2. breakoutholidays.co.uk (Breakout Holidays Ltd)
- Near-identical clone of FlyFast (same template, same Great Portland St address, same form, same grid). Services incl. "visa refusal review and support". Flights page = 12 listings to enquiry. Sitemap exposes e-visa content type + members/subscription area (empty).
- Packages: same 5 cards with uniform 4.8/5 stars + "Starts with - £X", no inclusions, CTA to /begin-your-application/. "Italy, 5 days, 4.8/5, £150"; "Switzerland, 6 days, 4.8/5, £205".
- Visa hook: /schengen-visa-assistance/ H1 "Schengen Visa Consultants in the UK". Tiers: "Consultation Only £60", "Appointment Process Guidance £90", "Complete Schengen Preparation Support £140". Explicit: "We have no special or priority appointment access."
- Sales: 11-field form (UK status, bank statement y/n, prior refusal y/n), WhatsApp wa.me/447490345711. /checkout-page/ renders empty. T&Cs: deposit "normally starting from £50 per applicant". 40%-off banner.
- Compliance (best of small operators): Breakout Holidays Ltd, company 16273801. "independent consultancy ... not part of, authorised by or affiliated with any government, embassy, consulate, immigration authority or visa application centre." KEY LINE: company is "not the organiser of a package holiday or the provider of transportation, accommodation or insurance" (explicit PTR opt-out despite "Holiday Packages" nav). Refund: full refund if cancelled before work starts; 14-day statutory cancellation acknowledged; third-party fees + completed appointment-booking fees non-refundable. "Fully UK GDPR compliant." No ATOL/ABTA.
- Markets: UK residents only.
- URLs: /uk-to-{country}-schengen-visa/ (8), /schengen-visa-assistance/, /holiday-packages/, /refund-and-cancellation-policy/. Membership plugin pages indexed (hygiene problem).
- Copy: PTR opt-out line + 14-day/refund-before-work policy + "no special or priority appointment access". Avoid: uniform fabricated-looking 4.8/5 on every card; indexed empty checkout pages.

## Synthesis
1. Package anatomy (FlyFast, Breakout, Tourloom): card grid, one destination, 5-10 days, 1-2 cities, hotel descriptor, 3 named sights, "booking confirmations", visa work bundled. Nobody itemises ground transport/transfers.
2. Price anchoring: two models only. FlyFast/Breakout "Starts with £150-£222" on cards that are really visa fees; everyone else enquiry-only with no £. Published visa fees: £60 / £69-£90 / £119-£140 per applicant, strike-through "31% OFF", £50-£60 deposit + balance on appointment.
3. Flights/ATOL: every "Flights" nav item is an enquiry form or dead link. Zero ATOL, ABTA, IATA across all six.
4. PTR: only Breakout addresses it, by opting out ("not the organiser of a package holiday..."). Tourloom sells hotel-inclusive packages with no PTR/ATOL/insolvency wording = biggest legal exposure visible.
5. Badges present: "not affiliated with government/embassy/VFS/TLS/BLS" on all six; company number on Tourloom, Breakout, IWEBRON (+VAT); IAA number only at IAS.
6. Badges missing: ICO number (none publish one), ATOL/ABTA/PTR protection (none), refund policy on the service page itself (T&Cs only), company number on FlyFast + SVC.
7. Non-UK source markets: only IAS (za./us. subdomains, /ng/ /ca/ /ie/ /es/ /pt/ /fr/, Lagos office). SA served from UK phone, no office. None target UAE. None sell Schengen packages to SA/UAE/USA/Canada residents: lane is empty.
8. Appointment promises: nobody says "guaranteed". Honest lines: Breakout "no special or priority appointment access", VisaD "We cannot create availability". Tourloom "Fast track appointment assistance" most aggressive.
9. Product framing rec: "visa-ready trips": {Country} · {N nights} · {cities} · hotel tier · ground transport NAMED (airport transfer, intercity rail) · "Schengen visa preparation included (£130 value)" · "Flights not included, you book your own, we match the itinerary". Mirrors Tourloom anatomy + the transport line nobody itemises.
10. Pricing rec: real "from £X per person (visa prep + hotel + transfers)" from actual hotel quotes, "indicative, confirmed on WhatsApp". Beats price-less Tourloom and misleading FlyFast/Breakout cards; avoids Google unclear-pricing flag.
11. Compliance block rec: locked disclaimer-strip + package line: "Beyond Passports arranges visa preparation, hotel and ground transport as separate services on your behalf; we do not sell flights and this is not an ATOL-protected package. Appointment availability is set by the visa centre." + CH 17331903 + ICO + refund rule. Get PTR view from compliance-research-agent before launch: hotel + transport for one trip can still be a "package" or "linked travel arrangement" under PTR 2018 even without flights.
12. International rec: copy IAS architecture (/za/ /ae/ /us/ /ca/ with local WhatsApp formats, local VAC names, local currency anchors, hreflang). No competitor sells Schengen visa-led trips to those markets: lead with the package card + honest "no flights, no ATOL" line.
