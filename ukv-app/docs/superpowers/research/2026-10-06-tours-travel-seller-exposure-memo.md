### 3.1 UK PTR 2018 (SI 2018/634) [P] legislation.gov.uk regs 2, 3, 4, Part 5, 26 (fetched 2026-10-06)
Reg 2 "package": "a combination of at least two different types of travel services for the purpose of the same trip or holiday, if (a) those services are combined by one trader ... before a single contract on all services is concluded; or (b) those services are (i) purchased from a single point of sale and selected before the traveller agrees to pay, (ii) offered, sold or charged at an inclusive or total price, (iii) advertised or sold under the term 'package' or under a similar term, (iv) combined after the conclusion of a contract by which a trader entitles the traveller to choose among a selection of different types of travel services, or (v) purchased from separate traders through linked online booking processes where (aa) the traveller's name, payment details and e-mail address are transmitted ... and (bb) a contract with the latter trader ... is concluded at the latest 24 hours after the confirmation of the booking of the first travel service."
Exclusion: a core travel service combined only with minor "other tourist services" is not a package if those "do not account for a significant proportion of the value ... and are not advertised as ... an essential feature". Does NOT help BP: hotel (limb (b)) + rail/transfer/ferry (limb (a)) are two listed core types.
"linked travel arrangement": "at least two different types of travel service purchased for the purpose of the same trip ... not constituting a package, resulting in the conclusion of separate contracts with the individual service providers, if a trader facilitates (a) on the occasion of a single visit to, or contact with, a trader's point of sale, the separate selection and separate payment of each travel service by travellers; or (b) in a targeted manner, the procurement of at least one additional travel service from another trader where a contract ... is concluded at the latest 24 hours after the confirmation of the booking of the first travel service."
"organiser": "a trader who combines and sells, or offers for sale, packages, either directly or through another trader or together with another trader; or the trader who transmits the traveller's data to another trader". "retailer": "a trader other than the organiser who sells or offers for sale packages combined by an organiser." "trader": any person acting for business purposes "in relation to contracts covered by these Regulations, whether acting in the capacity of organiser, retailer, trader facilitating a linked travel arrangement or as a travel service provider." "travel service": "(a) the carriage of passengers; (b) the provision of accommodation ...; (c) the rental of cars ...; (d) any other tourist service". "point of sale": "(a) any retail premises ...; (b) a retail website or similar online sales facility ...; or (c) a telephone service."
Reg 3 scope: packages and LTAs offered for sale or sold by traders to travellers; excludes <24h without overnight, occasional not-for-profit, business travel under general agreement. No territorial limit on traveller residence in fetched text.
Reg 4: where sold through a retailer, organiser and retailer must ensure regs 5-7 information duties are performed.
Reg 19: "The organiser of a package who is established in the United Kingdom must provide effective security to cover, in the event of the organiser's insolvency, the reasonably foreseeable costs of (a) refunding all payments ... (b) if the carriage of passengers is included ... repatriating the traveller". Regs 20-25: bonding (Business Companion: bond not less than 25% of estimated annual payments in the plain-bond case; 10% with approved body reserves), insurance, monies in trust, offences.
Reg 26 (tool paraphrase + fragments): LTA facilitator established in the UK must provide effective security for "refunding all payments the trader receives from travellers"; (4) extends to non-UK traders selling LTAs to UK customers; (7) before the traveller is bound, must state the traveller "will not benefit from any of the rights applying exclusively to packages" and "will benefit from insolvency protection"; (8) prescribed wording Schedules 6-10; (12) contracting trader must inform the facilitator.
Guidance: gov.uk "Package holidays: complying with regulations, guidance for businesses" (updated 25 Jul 2022, 39-page PDF) [X] binary undecodable; counsel to read. Pending reform: "Package travel legislation: consultation on proposed amendments" and "Package travel, updating the framework 2025" (updated 2 Dec 2025) [X]; counsel to check whether amendments are laid.

### 3.2 UK ATOL Regs 2012 (SI 2012/1017) [P] regs 4, 4B, 9, 9B
Reg 4: "flight accommodation" = "accommodation for the carriage of persons on flights". Reg 9: "A person must not in the United Kingdom make available flight accommodation unless that person is (a) the operator ... (b) an ATOL holder ... (c) exempt under regulation 10; or (d) exempted by the CAA under regulation 11." Reg 9B(1)-(2): must not "facilitate the making available of flight accommodation by another person" where flight is a component of a package of which P is organiser, or P acts in the course of business and facilitation "is not limited to arranging payment". Reg 4B extends to procuring/facilitating flight accommodation. CAA "Do I need an ATOL" [R]: "A business selling a flight-inclusive package ... or a Flight-Only must fall into one of the above categories." Silent on non-flight sales.

### 3.3 UK CTSI Business Companion "Package travel and holidays" (Jul 2025) [R]
Restates reg 2; LTA facilitators must give Schedule 6-10 information "in a clear, comprehensible and prominent manner" and hold "effective security". No discussion of a business recommending suppliers without taking payment.

### 3.4 UK DMCCA 2024 Part 4 Ch 1, CMA207, CAP Code [P][R]
s.251(1): "The Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008 are revoked." Ch 1 in force 6 Apr 2025. s.230(10) "invitation to purchase": "a commercial practice involving the provision of information to a consumer (a) which indicates the characteristics of a product and its price, and (b) which enables, or purports to enable, the consumer to decide whether to purchase". Material information includes "the total price" or, where it cannot reasonably be calculated in advance, "how the price will be calculated", with equal prominence. s.227(2) material information = "information that the average consumer needs to take an informed transactional decision." CMA207 (Nov 2025): "When a trader gives information to consumers about a product and its price, this will normally be an 'invitation to purchase'"; total price must include "any mandatory fees, taxes, charges or other payments"; "drip pricing ... is prohibited." CAP Code 3.17 price statements must not mislead by omission; 3.18 quoted prices include non-optional taxes/fees; 3.19 if a fee cannot be calculated, say so and how; 3.22 "'from' must not mislead by exaggerating the availability".

## 4. Analysis
### 4.1 UK PTR 2018
**Option (a) outside PTR reading:** no travel contract, no payment, no data transmission, generic referral; the operator is organiser, BP a lead source. "Targeted manner" (LTA (b)) needs a second contract within 24h.
**Option (a) still caught reading:** (i) limb (b)(iii): a card under "package"/"tour package" (route slug `/tour-packages`) is a package by definition; offering a partner's package = retailer, regardless of commission; reg 4 duties follow. (ii) limb (b)(ii): a combined "from £X" is "offered ... at an inclusive or total price"; nothing requires the trader be the payee. (iii) LTA limb (a): WhatsApp is a "telephone service" = point of sale; steering the client to supplier 1 then supplier 2 in one contact engages LTA (a) even with no payment; reg 26(7)-(8) information duties attach (security nil-value). (iv) "Commission" appears in no PTR definition; the regime turns on combining, selling, offering, transmitting data, facilitating. ZERO COMMISSION DOES NOT OF ITSELF TAKE THE ACTIVITY OUTSIDE THE PTR.
**Option (b) outside "package" reading:** contracts are client-supplier; BP not party, not paid; at most LTA facilitator with nil security; arguably traveller's agent.
**Option (b) is a package, BP organiser reading:** limb (b)(i) "purchased from a single point of sale and selected before the traveller agrees to pay": the deep dive 6f flow presents "one-screen indicative quote: hotel line + transport line" and the client then pays; both selected via BP's WhatsApp before paying for either = limb (b)(i) met on its face; separate contracts and separate payments do not rescue it (LTA (a) requires select-and-pay one, then select-and-pay the next). Limb (a) arguable if BP assembles the itinerary. Organiser = regs 5-7 duties, Part 3 performance liability, reg 19 security (bond/insurance/trust). Reg 19 applies to a UK-established organiser with no stated traveller-residence limit: the UK regime may follow BP into ZA/AE/US/CA sales. "Sells" undefined; organiser need not be payee. The deep dive 6d draft strip concedes this reading in consumer copy and should not go live until counsel chooses a position.
**Does wording matter?** Yes: limb (b)(iii) makes the term sufficient. Avoiding "package" removes one of five routes; (b)(i), (b)(ii), (a) remain. Slug `/tour-packages` and names like "Fusion Europe Tour" need review.
**Does "from £X" alone create a package?** A single hotel + transport figure is an inclusive price. Whether an enquiry-only indicative figure is an "offer" is open. Splitting into two per-service figures avoids an inclusive price but may still be an LTA invitation.
**Breakout wording:** a label, not a fact. PTR status turns on what the trader does; reg 26(7) and regs 5-7 impose positive duties a denial does not satisfy; a false denial may be a "misleading action" under DMCCA s.226. Defensible wording must describe mechanics (who contracts, who is paid, when).

### 4.2 UK ATOL 2012
All triggers tied to flights. No trigger on stated facts. Any travel margin must be in the headline, never an "internal line".

## 8. Recommended copy and process for counsel review (drafts)
Designed so that if counsel confirms option (a) is outside, the facts on the ground match.
**8.1 May appear on a card:** destinations, nights per city, season, named hotel tier, named transport type/class, what is not included; "Schengen application prepared by Beyond Passports; visa service fee quoted separately."; "Indicative itinerary. Hotels and transport are supplied and charged by the named suppliers, not by Beyond Passports."; CTA "Ask about this itinerary".
**8.2 Should NOT appear:** "package", "tour package", "holiday package", "deal", "bundle" anywhere in UK copy incl. slug `/tour-packages` and card titles (use "trip idea"/"itinerary"); a single combined "from £X" (if counsel permits prices, per-service supplier figures labelled "supplier's own price, indicative, checked {date}", mandatory extras named); "book", "reserve", "secure your trip", seat counters, deposits, strike-throughs; any flight reference beyond "Flights not included and not arranged by us."; any statement BP "arranges", "can arrange", "books", "organises" (CA, FL, OPC triggers): under (a) the verb is "we can introduce you to a licensed operator"; for Quebec no CAD pricing and no French content on trip pages until QC counsel clears.
**8.3 Enquiry handling, option (a):** visa qualification first; hand-off: "For the hotel and rail we can introduce you to [named licensed operator, with its own licence number]. You deal and pay with them directly; they are responsible for the trip and your money protection. We are not involved in that booking."; give the operator's public contact; do NOT transmit the client's name, payment details or e-mail; do not send booking links for two suppliers in one conversation; do not follow up within 24h of a booking to procure a second service; no commission/referral fee/margin of any kind, logged in Leads.
**8.4 Enquiry handling, option (b) (only if counsel accepts exposure):** assume a UK package unless the traveller selects AND pays supplier 1 before supplier 2 is discussed; even then LTA reg 26 duties likely apply; never book in BP's name; never hold/route client travel money; never collect a travel deposit.
**8.5 When a referral becomes "arranging" (bright lines):** BP (1) transmits traveller name/payment/e-mail to a supplier; (2) presents two travel services for selection before the client has paid for either; (3) receives any payment, commission or margin connected to travel; (4) books, holds or cancels a reservation in the client's or its own name; (5) sends targeted supplier links for a second service within 24h of a first booking; (6) advertises it "can arrange" or shows one inclusive price; (7) touches a flight in any way.
**8.6 Disclaimer (mechanics over denial):** "Beyond Passports Ltd prepares your Schengen visa application. We do not sell, book or take payment for hotels, transport or flights. Trip ideas on this page are illustrations only; any travel you book is with the supplier or operator you choose, on their terms and under their consumer protections." Drop the 6d sentence conceding PTR application unless counsel decides (b) is a package and wants that disclosure.
