# Customer portal research: competitor portal matrix and official-portal pain map

Date: 2026-10-06
Author: competitive-research agent (Beyond Passports)
Scope: HALF A = do the non-Atlys/non-iVisa competitors have any customer portal, account, tracker or app. HALF B = what the OFFICIAL intake portals (VFS, TLScontact, BLS, Capago, Prenot@mi, GVCW) force the customer to do, where they break, and what people complain about.
Excluded by brief: Atlys and iVisa (covered by other agents).
Method: WebSearch + WebFetch of each site's live homepage/service page, Trustpilot/Hellopeter/forum pages. Every row carries a source URL and the date seen. All pages were fetched on 2026-10-06 unless stated. Anything not confirmed on a live page is marked [unverified]. No numbers are invented; where a page did not show a figure, the cell says "not shown".
Decision-basis link: this report supports product goal G1 (monitoring + human) in `ukv-app/docs/product-goals-2026-10.md` and the locked rule "every decision competition-led, cite a named competitor".

### Matrix read-out
- 15 of 24 have NO account, NO tracker, NO upload, NO online payment: every UK Schengen agency except VisaD (FlyFast, Breakout, Tourloom, Flypass, xVisa, GoVisa, EuroPath, Aviva, WE ARE LONDONERS), The Visa Agent (SA), Regal (UAE), Dokument (USA), Toronto Visa Express [unverified]. WhatsApp + phone + email is the entire "portal". That is the finding.
- Only 4 have a real login that a visa customer uses: VisaHQ, VisaD, Global Visa Shop, AFC Holidays (plus Swift and schengenvisapro outside the Schengen UK lane).
- Only 3 show any status to the customer: VisaHQ (courier hand-offs), Global Visa Shop (Pending/Approved/Rejected), Travel Visa Pro (order logistics). None explains the OFFICIAL portal's status strings or the next action.
- Only 1 has a native app (AFC Holidays), and it is a travel-booking app.
- The two bots (Visard, Visa Catcher) solve one pain only (slot hunting), take Stripe payment, and run on Telegram, not a web account.
- Flypass actively argues against uploading documents to a website. A BP portal must answer that objection (UK-hosted, encryption, deletion on request, ICO registration ZC197159 already held).

## 2. Official portal pain map

Purpose: a BP portal sits BESIDE these portals. It does not replace them and must never hold or automate the customer's official credentials (TLScontact and Prenot@mi both treat automation as grounds for blocking; Visa Catcher itself avoids holding credentials). The portal removes the pain each step causes.

### Build-order suggestion (not a decision)
Ranks 1, 4, 5 and 9 are data + copy, no integration risk, and cover the four highest-frequency pains; they can ship as a read-only "My case" page before any upload or payment plumbing.
