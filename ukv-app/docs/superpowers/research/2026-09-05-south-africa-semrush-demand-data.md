## Payment flow check (cycle 10, 2026-09-05)

- **Spec/plan gap:** neither the SA design spec nor the phase1 plan addresses currency/payment method at all. `config('ukv.markets.za')` only holds `currency: 'ZAR'` and null price placeholders — no payment-flow decision exists anywhere.
- **Not a launch blocker:** currency is hardcoded to `'gbp'` in `StripeService.php` (3 places: `createCheckoutSession()`, `createChecklistSession()`, `createBespokeQuotePaymentLink()`), with no country-restriction logic anywhere in checkout. A South African customer can complete checkout **today, unmodified**, charged in GBP — their bank converts to ZAR on the statement, same as any international card payment.
- **Real decision needed before Task 5's copy locks in a ZAR price:** stay GBP-only (zero code changes, but the displayed ZAR price becomes illustrative-only — actual charge is GBP, converts to a different number + the customer's bank cross-border fee, ~2-3% typical) vs. add native ZAR charging (small, well-scoped Stripe change — VERIFIED via stripe.com/docs that ZAR is a supported presentment currency, min charge 0.50 ZAR — but currently unplanned and unbuilt).
- **Side finding:** Stripe routes South African merchants to Paystack (a separate Stripe-owned product) rather than offering direct SA accounts — doesn't affect BP (UK-based account charging an SA-located customer is fine), but rules out a future native SA Stripe account.
- **Awareness only, not Phase 1 scope:** SA consumers are used to instant-EFT options (Ozow, PayFast, SnapScan) alongside cards — flagging in case SA card-only conversion underperforms UK later.
- **3D Secure friction for SA-issued cards:** UNCHECKED, issuer-dependent, unresolvable without a live test transaction.

- **Process transfers, specific mechanism does not.** What carries over: never-sweep-gated-operators discipline, per-client-only booking, checking the destination embassy's own site to discover the operator (the method already used to confirm Germany=TLS-in-SA), and the standing §0c rule (never copy UK operator assumptions to a new market).

- **Germany-in-SA is a cold discovery problem, not a transfer:** Germany was never gated in the UK (VFS-operated there), so BP has zero prior exposure to any German-consulate gate mechanism. Nothing mechanical to reuse — only the meta-lesson to check for a government-portal-ref pattern as one hypothesis to test.

Every fact-bearing string (operator name, unlock mechanism, centres, currency, flag colors, compliance badge) is France/UK-specific — needs full re-verification per new country, not find-replace.
- **Architecture:** no new pattern needed — a future Germany-in-SA page would follow the same config-flag-gated pilot pattern already used for `/visa/france`.
