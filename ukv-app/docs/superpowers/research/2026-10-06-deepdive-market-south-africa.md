## 0. Headline findings
1. Pain is NOT "no appointments" at scale; it is SILENCE, nickel-and-dime fees, passport limbo. VFS Hellopeter TrustIndex 1.8/10; TLScontact SA 1.5 stars NPS -100; every operator "unclaimed, does not respond". Only Netherlands/VFS publishes a scarcity notice: "Appointments are currently limited."
2. Refusal rate LOW (~5.7% 2024). The viral "37.2%" figure is unsourced SEO (Kouamou Capital). Real: ~192,000 applications / ~11,000 refusals / $1.1m lost (2024, TRT Afrika); News24 5 Jun 2026 "up to R26m". Codera: France/Germany/Italy ~5%. Do not use "beat the 37% rejection rate".
3. CORRECTION TO VAC MAP: Switzerland in SA = VFS Global ("official partner of the Embassy of Switzerland in South Africa"), NOT TLScontact. Greece = GVCW, Spain = BLS, France = Capago, Germany + Belgium = TLScontact, Italy/NL/PT/AT/CH = VFS. Visalink = old Germany provider, current mandate unverified, drop from live map.
4. Price anchors: govt+VAC R2,100-2,700 (Spain cheapest: R1,775 + R333 = R2,108). Only transparent agency, The Visa Agent, R1,590 (FR/IT/CH). £130 = R2,858 = 80% above visible local anchor and above the government fee itself.
5. Every competitor hides pricing except The Visa Agent; none offers slot monitoring; trust currency = Google reviews (1,280 / 249) + WhatsApp, not Hellopeter or ASATA.

## 3. Document norms for SA applicants
- Bank statements: 3 months (Germany "money movements of the past three months"; Italy "each page must be stamped by the bank"; Greece "original ink stamp"); ≤1 month old. Red flags: "Sudden large deposits without explanation"; stability over months beats a single big balance.
- Daily means: France ~€65/day, Germany ~€45/day (indicative).
- Employer letter: original, letterhead, "position held, period of employment, exact dates of your leave", salary; 3 payslips. Self-employed: CIPC registration, business statements, accountant letter.
- Ties: employment, property, family, studies.
- Minors: "ORIGINAL UNABRIDGED birth certificate" + "certified letter of consent from the parent not travelling" (Italy); form signed by both parents (Germany, Switzerland). SA exit law: UBC "or equivalent parentage document"; DHA parental consent affidavit (valid 6 months, commissioner of oaths); passport with parent details exempts birth cert but airlines still ask. Under-12 biometrics exempt (Capago).
- Passport: 3 months beyond return (some agencies 6), within 10 years, 2 blank pages.
- Insurance: €30,000, Schengen-wide, whole trip; Italy wants insurer "one-page statement". ZAR price n/c (TIC -> Santam) [unverified].
- Photos 35x45 white; Italian checklist: "No teeth showing".
- SA passport "ZAF" place-of-birth quirk: not verified from official source; confirm with ops before FAQ.
- Refusal reasons (Visa Logistics guide): incomplete file, weak/unexplained finances, return doubts, invalid insurance, passport validity, wrong consulate (main destination), prior overstays. Rate "below 7%". Reapply immediately.

## 7. /za/ blueprint
**Positioning**: "Your Schengen file, checked line by line, with a named consultant on WhatsApp who actually answers. Price in rand, upfront." (Answers Theme A silence + E gatekeeping + The Visa Agent's "always in the loop".)
**Tiers / price construction (owner to pick; not published until confirmed)**: UK £130 = R2,858 (R879 + R1,979). Anchors: The Visa Agent R1,590; Capago all-inclusive ≈ R1,583; govt+VAC ≈ R2,100-2,300. SA consumer won't pay more for the agency than the visa without a visible reason. Hold deposit+balance, PPP-adjust total, let monitoring + named consultant justify the top tier:
- Essentials R1,490-1,590: operator checklist, form, appointment booked on applicant's own portal account, WhatsApp consultant. Matches The Visa Agent.
- Guided R2,290-2,490 (≈ £104-113): + daily slot monitoring where a public calendar exists (VFS, Capago, TLS), stamped-statement + minor-pack review, 48h pre-appointment mock check, post-decision read-back. Below govt+VAC, above The Visa Agent, below £130 parity.
- Family add-on R690 per additional applicant.
- Split: 30% on start (R690-750), balance on confirmed appointment (mirrors £40/£90). Deposits via Ozow/PayShap (1.5%); cards for balance.
- No "faster appointment" tier. "We watch the calendar so you don't have to", never "we get you a slot".
