## 3. Document norms for UAE residents
- Emirates ID front/back + residence visa page; residence valid 3 months beyond requested visa (Germany) / 90 days beyond return (Italy); agencies say 6 months.
- Passport: within 10 years, 2 blank pages, 3 months beyond exit (agencies 6).
- Employment: salary certificate and/or NOC on letterhead with stamp, signature, title, salary, approved leave dates, return confirmation. Classic NOC errors: missing stamp/signature, no leave approval. Self-employed: trade licence + company statements.
- Bank statements: 3 months (Germany) to 6 (agencies); STAMPED by bank; VFS: unattested statements = common error. Red flags: unexplained deposits, salary mismatch, dormant accounts.
- Money (agency guidance, no official minimum): salary "AED 3,500 or more" (Regal) / "3,000+"; balance "Dh15,000-20,000" average over 3-6 months (Visa Guy in Gulf News; Regal); daily EUR 50-100.
- Insurance EUR 30,000, all states, full duration; AED 56-250.
- Bookings: round-trip + accommodation; fake bookings risk blacklisting (Gulf News Oct 2025).
- Refusal reasons: insufficient financial proof (~21%), unclear purpose, weak UAE ties, inconsistent form, unattested statements, wrong main destination, undeclared past refusals.
- Timing: spikes before Eid Al Adha (26-29 May 2026) and summer (700 enquiries/day KT 2023); agencies: book 8-10 weeks early, or "three to four months in advance"; VFS extended Ramadan timings.

## 4. Payment and trust norms
- Fair-price anchors: legitimate fees AED 375-899 (Musafir 375-475 "appointment fee included", Akira 450, AFC 650, Green Apple 850, Regal 850, GVS 899, Rayna from $40.84 base, dnata AED 750 via reseller). Illegitimate: Dh2,500-4,000 "for a date". Official cost per adult AED 590-750 (consular + VFS + insurance + photos). Fee at/above govt cost reads premium; below AED 500 reads budget.
- BNPL: Tabby/Tamara accepted by GVS, Satguru, Whitesky, Pinoy; dnata ENBD 0% from AED 1,000. Tabby likely needs UAE-licensed merchant [unverified].
- Fraud warnings (trust copy): Dubai Police: transact "only through the relevant government authorities or licensed service providers", "Search the company's English and Arabic names in the National Economic Register", report via Dubai Police app / eCrime / 901 (KT 18 Jul 2026; The National 25 Aug 2026). Agent advice: landline, Dubai registration, website, pay in person.
- Reviews: Google dominates (Visa Guy "4.9, 1500+"; Green Apple 4.6 ~2,000 via Trustindex); Trustpilot Musafir 4.1; Tripadvisor Akira/Rayna.
- Licences: GVS "Commercial License Number: 1108940", "OutMazed Tourism L.L.C", "DTCM licensed"; The Visa Services "DMCC-977682"; AFC "DED & DTCM Licensed" no number; Akira, Regal, Visa Guy none. DET activity codes: 8299016 "Visa Services", 7910020 "Travel Agency Services"; setup guide (Antravia): "Offer tour guiding or visa services, you must hold a mainland tourism licence from DET"; fees AED 20,000-45,000. IATA TIDS = free supplier ID, not a consumer mark.
- Physical office: every competitor publishes a Dubai address; press tells applicants to check landline + visit.
- WhatsApp: text/voice notes/docs fine; voice/video calls blocked (TDRA VoIP, as of Aug 2026). Workaround: UAE landline callback (licensed telco / Botim / C'Me) or voice notes.
- PDPL Federal Decree-Law 45/2021: extraterritorial to controllers outside UAE processing residents' data (u.ae).
