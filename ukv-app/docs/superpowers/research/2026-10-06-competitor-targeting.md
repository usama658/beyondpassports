## 3. Destination x market targeting of the multi-market players (sitemaps, 2026-10-06)
**Atlys** (/{locale}/visa/{dest}-visa):
- en-GB: 106 destinations, 23 Schengen. MISSING: Italy, Spain, Portugal, Greece, Denmark, Iceland (six of the biggest UK Schengen demand countries).
- en-AE: 198 destinations, 28 Schengen (all but Denmark). Fullest Schengen coverage = UAE is their Schengen priority market.
- en-US: 105 destinations, 28 Schengen (missing Switzerland).
- en-CA: 76 destinations, ZERO Schengen.
- en-IN: 207 destinations, 26 Schengen.
- en-SA (Saudi): 21 Schengen. en-NG: 1. en-ZA: does not exist.
**iVisa** (/visas/{dest}/{origin}): 10 Schengen destinations per origin (Austria, France, Germany, Greece, Italy, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Spain, Switzerland) for UK, USA, Canada; 9 for UAE (no Spain). ZERO for South Africa. Long tail (Belgium, Czechia, Nordics, Baltics, Hungary, Croatia) untargeted everywhere.
**Visard** (/{market}/{dest}-...-in-{market}): UK 21 (no Belgium, no Greece, no Luxembourg, no Poland, no Sweden, no Czechia); UAE 11 (Austria, Bulgaria, Cyprus, Estonia, Finland, Hungary, Luxembourg, Malta, Norway, Portugal, Sweden: the LOW-demand set, none of France/Italy/Spain/Germany/Netherlands/Switzerland); USA 4 (Austria, Iceland, Netherlands, Portugal); Ireland 13; India 6.
**GoVisa**: no destination pages. **VisaHQ**: 29 country pages, source market only as ccTLD. **IAS**: Schengen as one page + nationality guides. **UK boutiques**: FlyFast 9 + Breakout 9 "uk-to-{country}" pages (FR, IT, ES, PT, BE, GR, CH, DE, +SE), no hub links. **Tourloom**: none.
