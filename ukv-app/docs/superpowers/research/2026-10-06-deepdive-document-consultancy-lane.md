## 3. Pricing mechanics
- Deposit/balance: Flypass partial payment + break clause (closest to BP £40/£90, split unpublished); Tourloom full before release; GlobalVisaShop/Visa Guy/VisaHQ/iVisa/Atlys full upfront; Aviva collects govt fee (holds client money).
- Refund triggers: Flypass "fees ... do not purchase an outcome"; Tourloom none on refusal; Visa Agent case-by-case if agent's fault; Aviva £120/£180 penalties; Visa Guy/VisaHQ 50% retention, 3-month card cutoff; CIBT forfeit at submission or 90 days; iVisa ladder 100/75/50/0; Atlys credits not cash, Protect on eligible routes.
- Strike-through: Atlys "₹1 visa" India sales only. Per-applicant everywhere; Aviva Minor/Spouse variants; no child service-fee discount anywhere.
- Express: CIBT +$301/+$440, Foreign Nationality +$79; Aviva express; Atlys/iVisa add-ons; Regal Nordics AED 1,250. UK consultancies none.
- Card fees: Aviva 6%.

## 4. Risky practices to avoid
1. Dummy "for visa purposes" bookings (Regal, Atlys "without any financial commitment", Green Apple, Visa Guy). BP: review real bookings only.
2. "Guaranteed" headlines (Atlys "Visas On Time Guaranteed"; EuroPath "Best prices guaranteed").
3. Fabricated stats (Visa Guy 98%/25,000; AFC 98%; One Visa World 99%/10,000; XVISA 95%; Akira "10 Days"). Only Atlys cites a basis. GlobalVisaShop's posture is right: "No agency can guarantee a visa."
4. Submission on behalf where not permitted (iVisa "Direct submission" vs VisaHQ "must be submitted by the applicant in person"); Green Apple's 59-month rule is UAE-VAC-specific.
5. Holding government fees (Aviva £385 bundle; iVisa all-inclusive; Visa Agent pays online fees): refund disputes + client-money exposure.
6. Copy-paste template errors (IAS "Home Office" on Schengen page; Visa Agent £1590/R1590/R1290). Add a price-consistency assert to BP's build.
7. Hidden terms (Breakout, FlyFast, EuroPath, Tourloom price, XVISA refunds, One Visa World, Visa Box). Flypass only UK peer publishing both "from" price and refund clause.
8. Card-age refund cutoffs (Visa Guy, VisaHQ, Aviva): Consumer Rights Act 2015 problems.
9. Missing ICO/company identity (Breakout, FlyFast, EuroPath). Only Flypass shows both.

## 5. Market-specific expectations
- UK: checklist, cover letter, itinerary, form, insurance guidance, audit, WhatsApp; client books (Flypass/Tourloom) or agency books (Aviva/XVISA); attends in person. Pain docs: BRP/eVisa share code, 3C leave, statement/payslip consistency, employer leave letter. Appointment scarcity = headline problem.
- UAE: form filling, cover letter, appointment booking, flight/hotel/insurance arranged, indexed pack, passport delivery; AED 450-899 + ~AED 400 embassy/VAC. Agencies are DTCM travel agencies. Pain docs: "Original NOC letter from employer or sponsor"; "Salary certificate and employment contract"; "Original bank statements with bank stamp (3-6 months)"; Emirates ID; residence visa valid "3+ months beyond departure"; tenancy contract; trade licence; translations. Quoted thresholds: "minimum monthly salary of AED 3,500", balance "AED 15,000 and 20,000". English + Arabic expected. UAE PDPL statement [counsel].
- ZA: forms, appointment at Capago/VFS/TLS, "all you need for your appointment", accompaniment, courier; flat R1,290-1,590 or bespoke. Pain docs: "Bank statements (last 3 months, stamped by the bank)"; employer letter with position, salary, approved leave; "Proof of Legal Residence" for non-SA passports; funds "~50-100 euros daily"; insurance €30,000; confirmed flights/accommodation before applying. Peaks Oct-Dec, Mar-May, Sep. POPIA; LPA memo exists.
- US: paid pre-check as discrete product (CIBT $89); full service $99 credit (VisaHQ) to $307 (CIBT) to $750 (Dokument); third-party submission only "where permitted"; rush + courier normal. Pain docs: green card / US residence visa valid "at least 3 months beyond departure"; F1 "Valid I-20, reference letter"; "6-month bank statements"; employer leave approval; tax docs; EU-spouse route. Spanish [unverified]. Seller-of-Travel if selling insurance/flights; CCPA [attorney].
- Canada: NO competitor fetched in this pass; all [unverified]: VFS most, BLS Spain; PR card/permit validity; EN/FR Quebec; PIPEDA + Quebec Law 25; TICO only if selling travel. (Covered by the US+CA market deep dive.)

## 6. Design recommendation for G2
### 6.1 Tier: "Schengen Document Pack + Named Consultant" (working name)
Included (all markets, localised examples): 1 eligibility + consulate-selection check (free, pre-payment); 2 profile-specific checklist (employee/self-employed/student/dependant/retired + market status-proof line); 3 completed form (client checks + signs); 4 cover letter; 5 day-by-day itinerary matched to real bookings; 6 accommodation + flight proof REVIEW (no reservations created); 7 insurance guidance (what the policy must say; client buys); 8 line-by-line audit with written corrections; 9 appointment booking help on official portal with the client, client's own account; 10 appointment-day pack (ordered file, what-to-bring, what-to-say); 11 named WhatsApp consultant until decision + tracking.
Excluded (printed): govt fee EUR 90/45/free + VAC fee paid by client at appointment; flight/hotel/insurance purchases; attending/submitting on behalf (client attends everywhere, incl. US); appeals/legal representation.
Add-ons (priced separately, TBD): refusal review + re-application plan; second household applicant reduced; express handling (our speed only).
"We do / You do" two-column block. Beats Aviva opaque bundle, iVisa "Direct submission" ambiguity, UK peers publishing nothing.

### 6.2 Fee presentation block (local currency)
1 "Our service fee, all-in": UK £130; UAE/ZA/US/CA TBD (anchors: AED 450-899; R1,290-1,590 or bespoke; $99-750, $307 mid; CA none verified). Do not publish until set.
2 "Payable now" £40 / "Payable once your appointment is secured" £90 (same trigger every market; only Flypass hints at it, publishing beats them).
3 "Government visa fee, paid by you at the appointment": EUR 90/45/free + local approx, dated.
4 "Visa centre fee, paid by you": varies (VFS/TLScontact/BLS/Capago/GVCW); exact only on verified country page.
5 "Apply directly instead": "You can apply without us on the official portal for the government and centre fees alone. Our fee is for preparation and review only."
6 Identity strip: Companies House 17331903; ICO ZC197159 (confirm from certificate PDF); market data-law line (UK GDPR / UAE PDPL / POPIA / CCPA / PIPEDA) [counsel].
Per-applicant; child service fee [decision].

### 6.3 Refund rule text (draft)
"What you pay us is for our work, not for the decision. The decision belongs to the consulate alone.
Payable now (£40): refunded in full if we tell you, after the free eligibility check, that we cannot take your case. Once we have started your checklist, form or letter it is not refundable.
Payable on appointment (£90): due only when an appointment date is confirmed. If no appointment can be secured before your travel date and you choose to stop, you owe nothing further.
Refusal: our fee is not refunded on refusal, because the work has been delivered. If the refusal letter cites an error that was ours, we redo the file for your re-application at no charge.
Government and visa-centre fees are paid by you directly and are never refunded by the consulate, whatever the outcome.
Your statutory rights are not affected."
Beats Tourloom, Aviva, Visa Guy/VisaHQ cutoffs, CIBT 90-day forfeit; stays honest vs Atlys/iVisa refund-on-rejection (do not imitate without insurance backing).

- Govt/VAC shown separately, never collected: Aviva, iVisa, The Visa Agent.
- "Apply directly instead": everyone.
- No dummy reservations: Regal, Green Apple, Visa Guy, Atlys.
- No success-rate/guarantee claims; odometer stats only: Visa Guy, AFC, One Visa World, XVISA, Atlys.
- Client attends; "We do / You do": iVisa, VisaHQ, Green Apple.
- CH + ICO + data-law strip: all UK peers except Flypass; all UAE/ZA/US sites.
- Refund tied to work stages, no card-age cutoff: Visa Guy, VisaHQ, CIBT, Aviva.
