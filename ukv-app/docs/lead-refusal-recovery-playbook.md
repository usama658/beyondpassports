# Lead Playbook — Schengen case-check & refusal-recovery (WhatsApp)

How to handle an inbound "check my case / my visa was refused" lead for **Beyond Passports**.
Applies to UK-based applicants for **Schengen** visas. Log every lead in the **Leads** tab of the
appointments Google Sheet.

> **Golden rules (never break):** We are an independent visa consultancy, **not the government**.
> We prepare + check the application and book the biometric appointment; **the consulate makes the
> decision**. Never guarantee an outcome or a date. Never tell someone to hide a prior refusal.
> Never advise applying to a country just because it has a nearby/earlier slot ("visa shopping") —
> that is itself a refusal risk.

---

## 1. First reply (within minutes) — acknowledge + qualify

Keep it calm and human. Do **not** promise anything yet. Collect the 5 intake facts:

1. **Nationality**
2. **UK status** (student / Graduate-PSW / Skilled Worker / spouse / ILR etc.)
3. **Passport expiry** (must be valid ≥3 months beyond return, issued within last 10 years, 2 blank pages)
4. **Trip dates + the country where they'll spend the MOST days** (this decides where to apply)
5. **If refused before: a photo of the refusal letter** + which country refused

Soft-commit line that converts without over-promising:
> "Thanks, let me look into it. Would you like me to connect you with a specialist who can guide
> you through the best strategy for your next steps?"

Then log the lead and arrange the callback.

**Document intake address:** ask clients to email documents to **`documents@beyondpassports.co.uk`**
(forwarder → the `hello@` master inbox). Filter/label the inbox by `To: documents@` for a clean
"case docs" view. Save each lead's docs into their folder under `Beyond Passports - Leads/` in Drive.

## 2. Reading a Schengen refusal letter (the 17 boxes)

The letter ("REFUSAL / ANNULMENT / REVOCATION OF VISA") ticks one or more reasons. Fixability varies:

| Box | Reason (short) | Meaning / fix |
|-----|----------------|---------------|
| 1 | False/forged travel document | **Serious.** Fraud finding. Do not touch; refer to a solicitor. |
| 2 | Purpose/conditions of stay not justified | Weak/absent purpose evidence. Fixable: build a clear purpose + itinerary + docs. |
| 3 | Insufficient means of subsistence | Money. Fixable: stronger, consistent bank statements + sponsor if any. |
| 4 | Can't lawfully acquire funds | Money/income credibility. Fixable with proper income evidence. |
| 5 | Already stayed 90/180 days | Overstay of allowance. Must wait until eligible again. |
| 6 | SIS alert (refusal of entry) | **Serious.** A member state flagged them. Needs specialist/legal review. |
| 7 | Threat to public policy/security | **Serious.** Legal review. |
| 8 | Public-health threat | Rare. Context-specific. |
| 9 | Threat to international relations | **Serious.** Legal review. |
| **10** | **Info on purpose/conditions "not reliable"** | **Credibility of the trip.** Very common + recoverable: rebuild a consistent, corroborated purpose story (cover letter + itinerary + bookings + funds all matching). NOT money, NOT fraud. |
| 11 | Doubts about reliability of statements | Similar to 10 — consistency + evidence. |
| 12 | Doubts about authenticity of documents | Document credibility. Fixable with verifiable, original docs. Careful. |
| 13 | Doubts about intention to leave before expiry | **Return-intent.** Strengthen ties to UK/home (job, status, family, property). |
| 14 | Border-application justification missing | Niche (visa-at-border). |
| 15 | Airport transit purpose not justified | Transit visa specific. |
| 16 | No valid travel medical insurance | Easy fix: Schengen-wide cover, min €30,000. |
| 17 | Revocation requested by holder | N/A. |

**Appeal window:** usually stated on the letter (e.g. Greece = 90 days, must be written in Greek via a
Greek lawyer). Appeals are **slow** — for a near-term trip, **reapplying with a rebuilt file is the faster route**.

---

## 3. The two questions every refusal case turns on

Most recoverable refusals (boxes 2, 10, 11, 13) come down to:
- **(a) Is the purpose genuine and internally consistent?** — cover letter, day-by-day itinerary,
  hotel + return flights, and funds must all tell the *same* story.
- **(b) Will they return?** — return-intent evidence: employment/contract, immigration status,
  family/property ties, enrolment, onward commitments.

**Temporary UK statuses (PSW/Graduate, student, short work) get extra return-intent scrutiny.**

---

## 4. Apply to the correct country (anti visa-shopping)

- Apply to the country of the **main stay** (most days). If equal, the **country of first entry**.
- Applying to the "wrong" country for the trip is a classic refusal trigger — do **not** let a client
  pick a country just because its centre is nearer or has an earlier slot.
- A refusal from one country does **not** ban applying again (to that or another country); the new
  application must **declare** the prior refusal.

---

## 5. Timeline feasibility (be honest)

- Standard processing: **up to 15 working days**, sometimes longer, from the biometric appointment.
- Work backwards from the trip: appointment must usually be **~1–2 weeks before travel** to leave
  room for processing + passport return.
- Check the soonest appointment (see the appointment sweep / Update Availability). If the maths
  doesn't work, **say so** — don't take money for a trip that can't realistically make it.

---

## 6. What a strong rebuilt file contains

- Completed short-stay (Type C) form
- Passport (valid, 2 blank pages) + prior visas
- 2 Schengen-spec photos
- **Cover letter** tying the whole trip together (and briefly, honestly addressing the prior refusal)
- **Day-by-day itinerary** matching the bookings
- Hotel + **return** flight bookings aligned with the exact dates
- **Proof of funds** — recent bank statements, consistent with the trip
- **Return-to-UK evidence** — job/employment, immigration status, ties
- **Travel medical insurance** — Schengen-wide, min €30,000
- Proof of UK residence/status (for non-British applicants)

## 7. Positioning + fee

- Refusal-recovery + tight timeline = the **Advanced / bespoke** tier (manual review, refusal-ground
  analysis, cover-letter strategy).
- Confirm scope + fee on the call. The service fee is **separate** from the consulate/VFS fee and
  does not guarantee approval.

---

## 8. Do-say / Don't-say

**Do say:** "the consulate decides"; "one reason was ticked, here's what it means"; "I'll be honest
about whether the timeline is realistic"; "you must declare the previous refusal."

**Don't say:** "we'll get you the visa"; any guaranteed approval or date; "we can speed up the
government decision"; anything implying we're official/government; advice to conceal a refusal or
apply to the wrong country.
